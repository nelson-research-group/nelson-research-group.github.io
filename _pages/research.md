Synthetic polymers are produced at enormous scale, with a growing need for more sustainable materials  is designed to be biodegradable. Whether a material actually degrades in the environment, and how quickly, depends on its chemical structure, the material's form, and the conditions it meets. My research quantifies these processes with analytical tools that can follow polymer carbon directly.

Macromolecular Analytical Chemistry {#analytical}
------
We work to develop new analytical approaches to study the breakdown of polymers in the environmental. Examples include <sup>13</sup>C labeling together with stable isotope probing (SIP) of CO<sub>2</sub> and microbial biomass during biodegradation, and NMR diffusion-ordered spectroscopy (DOSY) techniques to study macromolecular changes to polymers upon e.g., photoirradiation.

Publications: 
1. **Nelson, T. F.**\*; Ward, C. P. [Diffusion-ordered spectroscopy for rapid and facile determination of consumer plastic molecular weight](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.analchem.3c00655). *Analytical Chemistry* **2023**, *95*, 8560–8568.
1. **Nelson, T. F.**; Baumgartner, R.; Jaggi, M.; Bernasconi, S. M.; Battagliarin, G.; Sinkel, C.; Künkel, A.; Kohler, H.-P. E.; McNeill, K.; Sander, M. [Biodegradation of poly(butylene succinate) in soil laboratory incubations assessed by stable carbon isotope labelling](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-33064-8). *Nature Communications* **2022**, *13*, 5691.

Environmental Fate of Consumer and Novel Plastics {#envfate}
------
We study how sunlight degrades consumer plastics at the sea surface, and how formulation choices and biofouling change that fate.

Publications:
1. Walsh, A. N.; Mazzotta, M. G.; **Nelson, T. F.**; Reddy, C. M.; Ward, C. P. [Synergy between sunlight, titanium dioxide, and microbes enhances cellulose diacetate degradation in the ocean](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.2c04348). *Environmental Science & Technology* **2022**, *56*, 13810–13819.
1. **Nelson, T. F.**\*; Reddy, C. M.; Ward, C. P. [Product formulation controls the impact of biofouling on consumer plastic photochemical fate in the ocean](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.1C02079). *Environmental Science & Technology* **2021**, *55*, 8898–8907.
