Beyond research articles, I contribute to the scientific community and to public conversations about plastics and the environment.

Events
---------

I am co-organizing the 2nd edition of the **Conference on Polymers by European Early-career Researchers (PEERs)**, taking place in July 2027 in Munich. If you are an early career researcher in polymer science based in Europe, apply to join us! Find more info at the [conference website](https://uni-konstanz.de/peer-2027)



Research in the news
----------

I had the chance to communicate our work on carbon stable isotope labeling for studying polymer biodegradation in the environment for the [Nature Research Communities site](https://communities.springernature.com/posts/improving-our-understanding-of-polymer-biodegradation-in-soils-using-stable-isotope-labelling).
Our work was covered [by ETH Zürich](https://ethz.ch/en/news-and-events/eth-news/news/2022/10/accurately-tracking-how-plastic-biodegrades.html).
