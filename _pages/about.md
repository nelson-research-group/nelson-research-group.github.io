I am an environmental chemist at the [Department of Chemistry, University of Konstanz](https://www.chemie.uni-konstanz.de/), where I hold an Academic Council position. My research asks **what happens to polymers once they reach the environment**: how fast they break down in soils and the ocean, which chemical structures make them degradable, and how that knowledge can guide the design of plastics that are both useful and environmentally benign.

My work combines polymer chemistry with environmental and isotope geochemistry. I use stable carbon isotope (<sup>13</sup>C) labelling to follow polymer carbon as microorganisms convert it into CO<sub>2</sub> and biomass, and I study how sunlight drives the breakdown of consumer plastics at the sea surface. Together with polymer chemists, I help design and test new polyethylene-like materials that keep the properties of conventional plastics while staying recyclable and biodegradable.

Research interests
======
* Biodegradation of synthetic polymers in soils and natural environments
* Stable isotope labelling to trace polymer carbon into CO<sub>2</sub> and microbial biomass
* Photochemical degradation of plastics in the ocean
* Design of degradable and recyclable polyethylene-like polyesters
* Linking polymer chemical structure to environmental fate

Background
======
I received my Dr. sc. in Environmental Chemistry from **ETH Zürich** (2020), an M.Sc. in Environmental Science and Engineering from **Stanford University** (2015), and a B.A. in Chemistry from **Whitman College** (2014). I was a postdoctoral researcher at the **Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution** (2020–2022) and then an **Alexander von Humboldt Postdoctoral Fellow** at the University of Konstanz (2022–2024).
